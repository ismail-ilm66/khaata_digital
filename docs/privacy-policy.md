# Kharcha privacy policy

Effective 7 October 2026.

Kharcha is a personal expense tracker. It is built so that your financial records stay with you.

## The short version

- You don't need an account. There is no sign-up and no login.
- Everything you record stays on your phone.
- Kharcha has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to back it up, export it or email us.

## What Kharcha stores, and where

Your accounts, entries, budgets, people (udhaar), categories, settings and receipt photos are stored in Kharcha's private storage on your phone. We, the makers of Kharcha, never receive them.

If you turn on app lock, your PIN is stored only as a one-way, salted hash on your phone. Fingerprint and face unlock are handled entirely by your phone's operating system; Kharcha only learns whether the check passed.

## When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to:

- **Backup to a file.** The backup file goes wherever you save it. You can protect it with a passphrase (AES-256 encryption); without the passphrase, nobody, including us, can open it, and a forgotten passphrase can't be recovered.
- **Google Drive backup.** If you connect Google Drive, Kharcha signs in with Google and stores backups in a "Kharcha Backups" folder in your own Drive. It asks only for access to the files it creates, so it can't see anything else in your Drive. Google's privacy policy applies to your Google account and Drive.
- **Export.** Excel and CSV exports go wherever you save or share them.
- **Contact us.** If you email us from the app, the email includes a short technical report: the app version, whether the database passed its health check, the date of your last backup, and your phone's operating system version. It contains no amounts, names or notes, and you can read it before sending.

## Permissions

- **Camera and photos:** only when you attach a receipt.
- **Notifications:** only for bill reminders you turn on.
- **Fingerprint or face:** only if you turn them on for app lock.
- **Internet:** only for Google Drive backup.
- **Files:** only through the system's own file picker, when you import, export, back up or restore.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Kharcha, or clearing its data, deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved, and backups in your Google Drive, stay where you put them until you delete them.

## Children

Kharcha is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date, and the app's "What's new" notes will mention it.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: use **More → About → Contact us** in the app, or email the address published on Kharcha's Google Play listing.
